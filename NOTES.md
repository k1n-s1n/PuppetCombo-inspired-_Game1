# Project notes

Running log for the report: scripts written and third-party assets used (with sources).

## Scripts

| Script | Location | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `FirstPersonController.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | `Teleport()` and `LookAt()` helpers for scripted moments. WASD movement, mouse look, Shift to walk faster, using a CharacterController (collides with walls and slides along them, follows slopes, gravity). Mouse sensitivity, speeds and look limit are exposed in the Inspector. Uses the Input System package (`Keyboard.current` / `Mouse.current`). Esc frees the cursor, clicking recaptures it. Finds the child camera automatically if the Inspector slot is empty. |
| `IInteractable.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | Interface ("contract") for anything usable with E: `GetPrompt()` and `Interact()`. Doors now; pickups/keys later. |
| `PlayerInteractor.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On the Player. Raycasts from the camera centre each frame (2.5 m reach); shows "[E] prompt" and a crosshair dot via OnGUI; E calls `Interact()`. `ShowMessage()` shows temporary text (e.g. "It's locked."). Ignores objects whose prompt is empty (not usable yet). The ray uses `Physics.DefaultRaycastLayers` and the Player is on the Ignore Raycast layer, so the ray never hits the player's own capsule when looking down. |
| `NightSky.shader` | `Assets/_Project/Shaders/` | Custom BRP skybox shader (material `_Project/Materials/NightSky.mat`). Per-pixel gradient (horizon to zenith), procedural twinkling stars (random stars in a 3D grid of cells, each with its own brightness, tint and twinkle timing) and a moon disc with glow. Also fades the horizon into the scene's fog colour (`unity_FogColor`), because Unity never fogs the skybox: without this, gaps such as the end of the road showed a dark hole in the fog. All values tweakable on the material. Hash functions from Dave Hoskins' "Hash without Sine" (MIT, https://www.shadertoy.com/view/4djSRW). |
| `Door.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | Swings a door open/closed around its hinge pivot, always away from the player. Optional linked door (double doors), `isLocked` + locked message (key support to come). Angle and speed in the Inspector. On the 5 store doors. |
| `GameUI.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | Story UI singleton (`GameUI.Instance`): queued subtitles in a speech bar at the bottom (`Say(speaker, text)`), objective text at the top on a dark strip, flashing when changed (`SetObjective`), and dialogue choices answered with number keys (`ShowChoice`, `ChoiceResult`). Drawn with OnGUI so it sits above the VHS effects. On the `GameUI` object in Main. |
| `StoryManager.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | Runs Chapter 1's story beats in order as a simple state machine (`Stage` enum). Other scripts report events with `StoryManager.Instance.OnEvent("...")`; it only reacts if the event fits the current stage. Plays the opening subtitle after a short delay (coroutine). New objectives wait until the current subtitles finish (`SetObjectiveAfterSpeech`), and are skipped if the story moved on meanwhile. Shopping objective shows a count, e.g. "Grab snacks and beer (1/3)". On the `StoryManager` object. |
| `StoryTrigger.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | Invisible trigger box: when the Player (tag `Player`) walks in, sends its `eventId` to the StoryManager. `Trigger_EnteredStore` sits just inside the front doors. |
| `ToiletUse.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | Toilet aiming minigame on the `Toilet` object (only usable at "Use the toilet"). E moves the player to `StandPoint` and looks down at the bowl (new `Teleport`/`LookAt` helpers on FirstPersonController). A cartoony stream is drawn with a LineRenderer as a parabolic arc from waist height to the aim point. The mouse moves the aim on a flat plane at rim height; Perlin noise makes it wobble so the player keeps correcting. The bar fills only while the aim is within `bowlRadius` of `BowlCentre` (a dark `BowlWater` disc marks the target); misses raycast down and leave small yellow puddle discs (max 30). When full: "What a mess... not my problem, heh." and the story moves on. Difficulty (wobble, fill time, radius, sensitivity) in the Inspector. |
| `ShoppingBasket.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On the top basket of the stack by the entrance. Usable at the "Grab a basket" stage: E parents it to the camera (held in the lower right, tilted so you can see inside; position/rotation in the Inspector), removes its colliders and turns on its fill light. `ShoppingBasket.Held` is the held basket; `AddItem()` moves a collected item to that item's own spot inside it, so the layout is the same whatever order items are collected in. |
| `ShopItem.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On each item to collect (chips, beer, candy bar). Each has its own `positionInBasket`/`rotationInBasket`. E puts it in the held basket, or says "I should grab a basket first." The StoryManager counts all ShopItems in the scene, so adding one extends the shopping list automatically. |
| `Cashier.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On the (placeholder) cashier. At "Pay for your items", E starts the checkout conversation as one coroutine: basket goes on the counter, he asks your name, dialogue choice (tell him -> missing-people warning; refuse -> "You'll get what's coming for ya" and silence), ID card shown until E, then the basket is swapped for a paper bag in your hand and the story moves to "Head back to your car". Freezes the player during the conversation. |
| `IDCard.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On the GameUI object. Draws the student ID card (OnGUI) with name, **student number 33010198**, date of birth, course; all fields editable in the Inspector. Text scales with the screen size. |
| `HeadLook.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On the cashier, customer and jumpscare characters. In LateUpdate (after animation), turns the Humanoid head bone towards the player's camera while the player is inside the character's field of view and range; yaw/pitch clamped to a natural range, smoothly blended in and out. Without an animation playing it resets the head to its rest pose each frame so turns don't add up. |
| `Jumpscare.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On the redneck. Triggered when the player walks out of the toilet: he rushes from the shop floor to just in front of the camera in 0.3 s as a black silhouette (MaterialPropertyBlock tint), the camera shakes (optional sting sound), the shadow fades off, he complains, then walks through a path of points into the toilet (opening the door if shut), disappears and the door shuts. Player frozen until he's done talking. Afterwards the toilet door is locked with the message "Can't go in. The creep's still in there." |
| `ArmPose.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On the cashier. Lowers the arms out of the model's T-pose once at Start by rotating the Humanoid upper-arm bones around the body's forward axis (and bending the elbows slightly). Does nothing if an Animator Controller is assigned (an animation then poses the arms). |
| `CarEnding.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On the player's car. At "Head back to your car", E ("Drive off") freezes the player, hides the HUD, fades to black (OnGUI) and shows "END OF CHAPTER 1 / To be continued...". Earlier, it just says the player can't leave without the snacks. |
| `CustomerWander.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | On the customer. Walks a loop of route points (no pathfinding; points placed so straight lines stay in the aisles). At browse points she turns to face the shelf (the point's forward direction) and stands for a randomised time; others are walked through. Stops and waits if the player is standing close in front of her. |
| `ObjectiveSparkle.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | Twinkling star that marks objective items (a PS1-horror convention so items aren't lost on the shelves). On a child of each item and the basket: a camera-facing quad (billboard) with our `Sparkle.png` star texture (32 px, generated with a short Python script) and additive `Sparkle.mat`; every 1.6 s it grows, spins and fades (sine curve). Only shown during listed story stages (basket: "Grab a basket", items: "Grab snacks"); hidden when picked up (`ShopItem.HideSparkles`). |
| `SimpleWalkCycle.cs` | `Assets/_Project/Scripts/` | Procedural walk animation (no clip): while moving, swings the Humanoid thigh bones like pendulums (sine wave), bends each knee as its leg swings forward, swings the arms opposite; blends in/out when starting/stopping. Resets the bones to their rest pose each frame. Does nothing if an Animator Controller is assigned. |
| `TextOnSurface.shader` | `Assets/_Project/Shaders/` | BRP shader for 3D TextMesh text (licence plates). Unity's default font shader draws through walls; this one depth-tests like normal geometry, uses the font texture's alpha for letter shapes, and applies scene fog. Material `Plate_Text.mat`. |

## Scene setup (not scripts, but worth mentioning in the report)

- Night lighting: directional light renamed `Moonlight` (dim blue, soft shadows, aimed from the sky shader's moon direction); Trilight ambient (dark blue); exponential fog matching the sky's horizon colour so the treeline fades into the sky.
- `Lighting` object in the Hierarchy: 8 cool-white ceiling point lights + 2 canopy lights in the store (placed where the original Blender area lights were), 7 orange sodium-style spotlights in the street lamps. No shadows on these, for performance.
- Quality setting Pixel Light Count raised 4 -> 6 so the large shop floor is lit evenly.
- Developer's lighting pass: flat ambient (~0.5 grey) for readability, grey exponential-squared fog (density 0.03), dimmer shop lights, white street lamps.
- Post Processing Stack v2 (Unity package `com.unity.postprocessing` 3.5.4): Post-process Layer on PlayerCamera reading the `PostProcessing` layer; global `Post Processing Volume` object with profile `_Project/Scenes/Main_Profiles/PlayerCamera Profile.asset`. Effects used: Ambient Occlusion, Color Grading, Lens Distortion, Bloom, Vignette. Post Processing runs before the Unity_PostEffect VHS scripts (RGBShift, Posterize, Scanlines, BadTV), so the tape effects sit on top.

- Shopping props (our own, built from Unity primitive shapes with flat-colour Standard materials in `_Project/Materials`, saved as prefabs in `_Project/Prefabs`): `ShoppingBasket` + `BasketDeco` (red plastic basket stack by the entrance), `Item_Chips` (Food Collection crisp bag standing upright, aisle 1 middle shelf, scene scale 1.5), `Item_Beer` (carton: six Food Collection parody beer bottles in our blue carrier, on a stack of `BeerCase`s in front of the drinks fridges, scale 1.4), `Item_Candy` (Food Collection chocolate bar standing upright on the counter, label facing the shop, scale 2.5). Baskets scaled 1.3 to fit; in the basket the crisps lean against the far side with the chocolate bar across in front and the beer carton on the right; the held basket has a small point light (`BasketFillLight`) switched on while held so the contents stay visible. All under the `Shopping` object in the scene.
- Checkout (`Checkout` object): `Cashier_Placeholder` prefab (capsule body, sphere head on a separate `Head` object for head tracking later, dark eyes), `BasketSpot` on the counter, hidden `PaperBag` prefab. Placed on the open part of the counter, clear of the candy rack.
- `Trigger_LeftStore` just outside the front doors plays "That guy gave me the creeps." after checkout.
- Player and the story triggers are on the Ignore Raycast layer so they never block the E interaction ray.
- Characters (`Characters` object): `Customer_Girl` wandering a 9-point `CustomerRoute` (browses aisle 1, aisle 2 and the drinks fridges; kinematic Rigidbody on her moving collider; stares as you pass), `Jumpscare_Redneck` (hidden until the jumpscare) with `JumpscareStart` and `JumpscareWalkPath` points. The placeholder cashier's body was replaced with Character Male 01 (scale 0.9). `Trigger_LeftToilet` just outside the toilet door.
- `PlayerCar`: PSX Sedan parked nose-in towards the store beside the player's start, box collider, `CarEnding`. Front and rear `LicencePlate_*` objects: 0.52 x 0.26 m near-white backing (slightly emissive, like a plate light) with "HOLLOW PINES" and the student number **33010198** in black as TextMesh text (built-in LegacyRuntime font) using `Plate_Text.mat`.
- Characters have no animation clips: cashier uses ArmPose, customer uses SimpleWalkCycle. Mixamo clips could replace both later.
